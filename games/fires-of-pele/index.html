FIRES OF PELE

An On Island Time Adventure

This game needs a keyboard and mouse. Please open it on a Mac or PC.

Long Road Software

Paused

Progress also autosaves every dawn and at the end of each chapter.

How to Play

W A S DMove · Shift sprint · Space jump
MouseLook around · wheel zooms (arrow keys also turn the camera)
Left clickAttack / harvest trees, rocks, flax, berries, obsidian
Right clickSling a stone (once crafted)
1 2 3Club / Stone Spear / Obsidian Blade
EInteract: feed & tame animals, talk, collect, salvage
BBuild mode: LMB place · R rotate · U upgrade/repair · V demolish
CCrafting & inventions
F / G / HEat food / drink water / use bandage
PCompanions: follow you ⇄ guard the base
TRest at your Hearth until dusk
MToggle large map · Esc pause

Raiders come every night once your Hearth is lit, and each night is harder. Wood walls → stone → obsidian. Weaken ghost wolves to tame them; feed dogs and boars. Invent rain catchers, gardens and fish traps to stay fed.
