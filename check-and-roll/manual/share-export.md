## Share & Export Rig Lists – Team Collaboration

When you're ready, Check & Roll will let you share checklists with crew members or export them for backup.

### Sharing a Checklist

Once you've built one of your own rigs:
1. Tap the rig's options menu
2. Select "Share with team member"  
3. Enter their email address
4. Choose whether to sync changes automatically

<!-- [PHOTO] Share dialog showing email field and permission settings for auto-sync -->

### Exporting to File

For backup or printing:
- Export to CSV for spreadsheet programs
- Generate a PDF for quick reference on paper
- Save as image if you just want the checklist diagram stored

<!-- [PHOTO] Export menu showing CSV, PDF and PNG options at bottom of screen -->

### Apple Watch Companion – Reminders on Your Wrist

When sharing and exporting work lands:
- The watch app shows notifications for incomplete checklists  
- Tapping a notification opens the full rig in Check & Roll  
- You can mark items complete from your wrist
