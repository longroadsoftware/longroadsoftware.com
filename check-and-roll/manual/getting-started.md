## Welcome to Check & Roll

Check & Roll turns your daily RV inspection into a simple, repeatable routine. You've got two ways to begin: adding your own rig or starting from the example templates.

{% include '_layouts/default.html' %}

### Your First Steps

1. **Add a Rig** – Create a new vehicle inspection routine
2. **Or Start from a Template** – Use one of our predefined checklists

The main home screen shows all your rigs as cards. Tap any card to begin its checklist.

<!-- [PHOTO] Home screen showing rig cards for demo data rig "2017 Ford F-150" and user's own rig -->

### Adding a New Rig

From the home screen, tap the `+` floating action button or find "Add Rig" in the menu.

You'll see forms to fill:
- **Rig Name** – Your RV, truck camper, or travel trailer name
- **Make/Model/Year** – Vehicle details (not required but helpful for search)
- **Primary Use** – Daily driver, storage rotation, special events

<!-- [PHOTO] Add Rig form with fields for name, make/model/year, and usage notes -->

Start with these basics. You can always add more information later.

### Starting from a Template

We ship a demo rig ("2017 Ford F-150") so you can see how the app works before customizing your own.

**Why templates help:**
- Shows you what steps are possible to add
- Gives you an example of critical vs optional items  
- Lets you study before adding your first real rig template

<!-- [PHOTO] Template rig card for demo data showing completed checklist flow -->

After opening the checklist, duplicate it and then replace our examples with your details.
