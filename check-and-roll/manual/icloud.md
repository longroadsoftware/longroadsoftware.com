## iCloud Sync – Keep Your Rigs in Sync

Check & Roll uses iCloud to keep all your rigs available on your Mac, iPad and iPhone. This section covers cloud storage and automatic syncing.

### Setting Up iCloud

1. **Open Settings** in Check & Roll
2. **Go to Cloud > iCloud Storage**
3. Choose whether to sync:
   - All rigs immediately
   - New rigs automatically  
   - Selective syncing for specific items

<!-- [PHOTO] iCloud settings toggle showing "Sync all devices" switch enabled, with list of rig names shown in cloud state -->

### How Sync Works

- **Automatic background sync** – Changes on one device appear on others within minutes
- **Demo data rigs sync too** – The example Ford F-150 and other demo rigs appear on all synced devices  
- **Your custom edits stay local-first** until someone else needs to see them

<!-- [PHOTO] Sync status indicator showing green checkmark with "all rigs in sync" message -->

### Checking Sync Status

From the home screen:
- Look for the cloud icon in the top right corner
- A spinner means syncing now
- The cloud name shows active connection (iCloud.com, your Apple ID)

<!-- [PHOTO] Top bar showing active iCloud link for user's Apple account -->

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Rig appears on iPhone but not iPad:**
1. Check that both devices have same iCloud sign-in
2. Tap Sync icon to force a refresh
3. Ensure you're not on a restricted iCloud+ plan that blocks app syncing

<!-- [PHOTO] Troubleshooting flow chart showing three steps with sync refresh icon active in step 2 -->

**Sync seems slow:**
- Large photo uploads can take time—check the cellular connection
- Wi-Fi ensures faster updates, especially for checklist photos
