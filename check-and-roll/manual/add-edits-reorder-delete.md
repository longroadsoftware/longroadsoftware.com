## Add, Edit, Reorder, Delete Checklist Steps

Each rig's checklist is made of individual steps (inspect tires, check fluid levels, verify cargo straps, etc.). This section shows how to manage them.

## Adding a Step

Tap `+` in the step list header or the "Add Step" button at the bottom. You'll see these fields:

- **Step Title** – What this item checks (e.g., "Check tire pressure")
- **Detail Notes** – Optional instructions the driver should follow
- **Category/Tag** – Group steps logically (safety, cargo, fluid levels)

## Editing a Step

Tap any step to edit its fields. Changes save automatically—no need to hit a save button.

<!-- [PHOTO] Two steps shown side-by-side: original vs edited (different category/tag) -->

## Reordering Steps

Drag steps up or down in the list, or use the move handles on each row. The order becomes your standard operating procedure sequence.

<!-- [PHOTO] Step list with drag handles showing fluid level steps moved before cargo inspection -->

## Deleting a Step

Tap the "•••" menu on any step and choose delete. Confirm before it's removed. You can always add the same step back with similar wording.

<!-- [PHOTO] Delete confirmation dialog appearing for tire check step removal -->

**Tip:** Start small—5 to 10 steps is plenty for a daily drive. Add more over time as you discover new checks that make sense.
