## Run a Checklist – From Start to Finish

This shows what happens when you open your rig and complete its daily inspection routine.

### Opening Your Rig

1. **Tap any rig card** on the home screen  
2. **The checklist opens immediately** with all steps visible but unchecked  
3. You can switch between view modes (list, detail, progress) if needed

<!-- [PHOTO] Main checklist view showing empty state: progress bar at 0% with first item being tire check -->

### Marking Items as Complete

- **Tap or slide each step** to mark it done
- Progress updates instantly in the top bar  
- A checkmark appears next to completed items

<!-- [PHOTO] Completed step showing blue background color and checkmark icon, other items still grayed out -->

### Skipping Forward (If Allowed)

For non-critical steps, you may skip ahead:
- Hold or long-tap a step to fast-forward it
- You won't lose data—just the "checked off" state changes

<!-- [PHOTO] Progress bar showing 50% completion in mid-checklist -->

### Completing the Entire Routine

When all items are done, you see the finish screen:
- Date and time stamped  
- All completed steps listed  
- Optional photo upload for the finished checklist

<!-- [PHOTO] Finish screen showing date/time, green check icon, summary of all 10 completed steps -->

**Pro Tips:**
- Complete checklists in a safe spot away from traffic or hazards  
- Review before marking all items complete so you catch missed details  
- If something fails, note it immediately—don't just mark as done
