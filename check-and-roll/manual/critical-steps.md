In plain words: Marking certain steps as critical so they cannot be skipped.

## Mark Critical Steps

Some checks can't be skipped—fluid levels, safety equipment, brake pads, etc. Mark them "critical" to lock the order and prevent users from skipping past these items.

### How It Works

- Toggle the checkbox on any step
- When a critical step is encountered, the app pauses for completion before allowing progress
- Critical steps are visually distinguished with an icon or background color

### Setting Up Critical Items

1. **Open your rig's checklist** – Tap any step
2. **Toggle the lock** – The red switch becomes active when this can't be skipped  
3. **Save changes** – The switch stays on in the saved checklist

<!-- [PHOTO] Critical mode shown with progress bar showing "brake pads" must complete before next item -->

### Common Critical Checks

Your rig might need:
- Brake pad thickness
- Main fluid levels (oil, coolant)  
- Air leak checks for RVs
- Safety gear verification  
- Cargo strap integrity

When a user skips past a critical item without checking it off, the app blocks forward progress until that check passes.
