## About & Support

This page shows version information, help contacts, and resources available to you.

### About Screen Location

Open **Settings > About** to see:
- App version number (1.0)
- Build details  
- "Help and Support" link that opens our contact page

Need more support? We've got resources:

### Privacy Note

Check & Roll keeps your rigs private. We store no data about you or your usage unless:
- You choose to enable an opt-in sharing feature  
- You sign up for any service through Settings > Privacy

Read [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) in full there.

### Reporting Issues

Found a bug? Tell us so we can fix it:
1. Note what happened (screenshot helps!)
2. Tap About > Report Issue or email our support address  
3. We typically reply within 24 business hours on weekdays

<!-- [PHOTO] Bug report form with fields for app version, device model and optional description -->

### Version History

Each build increment updates the **build number** only—the public App Store version stays 1.0 until we're ready to ship a new release (that's up to us). Hermes handles TestFlight uploads when needed.
