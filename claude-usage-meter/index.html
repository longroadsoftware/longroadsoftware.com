Claude Usage Meter

See how much of your Claude plan's usage limit is left, always in view at the top of your Mac's screen. It is free: no price, no trial, no ads, and no account with us.

Download free for Mac How to install

Version 1.0.1 · macOS 14 or later · signed and notarized by Apple

What it does

If you use Claude a lot, you find out you have hit your limit when Claude stops answering. The meter shows you beforehand.

  • Shows the same numbers as claude.ai › Settings › Usage, without opening a browser.
  • Shows percent remaining or percent used. You choose.
  • The ring turns orange, then red, as you run low.
  • Click it to see every limit on your plan (5-hour session, weekly, model-only caps) and when each one resets.
  • Can notify you when you drop below a level you set.
  • Optional Dock tile, and it can open at login.

Why it is free

We built it for ourselves. We use Claude every day, and we kept running into the weekly limit without warning. Once it worked, there was no reason to keep it to ourselves.

The source is public under the MIT licence, so anyone can read exactly what it does.

If it is useful to you, have a look at what else we make. That is all we ask.

Install

  1. Download the zip and open it.
  2. Drag Claude Usage Meter into your Applications folder, then open it.
  3. Your browser opens claude.ai. Click Authorize. The meter appears at the top right of your screen.

Sign in with the Claude account that has your paid plan (Pro or Max). If Claude Code is already signed in on the same Mac, the meter uses that and there is nothing to do.

Built for macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel. We run it every day on Apple silicon.

Trouble signing in? The troubleshooting notes cover the usual causes.

What it sends, and where

Your Claude sign-in goes only to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude. Nothing goes to Long Road Software or anyone else.

  • No account with us, no analytics, no crash reports, no update check.
  • Your sign-in is kept in your Mac's Keychain and is used only to read your usage.
  • To remove it, click the meter and choose Sign Out of Claude.

Unofficial. Claude Usage Meter is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Claude is a trademark of Anthropic. The app reads usage the same way Anthropic's own Claude Code does, and Anthropic could change that at any time.
