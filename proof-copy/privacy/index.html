Proof Copy

Privacy

The short version: Proof Copy collects nothing. It has no account and sends no usage data. This page says what little does leave your Mac, and only that.

What stays on your Mac

Proof Copy reads your camera card or folder, copies it, reads the copy back, and compares it byte for byte. The report and the receipt it writes stay in the copy, on your own drive. None of that — your files, their names, or what the report says — is sent anywhere. Checking a receipt later works the same way and does not need an account or a network connection.

Buying Proof Copy

Proof Copy comes in two editions, and each is bought a different way:

  • From the Mac App Store. Apple handles the purchase. We never see your payment details; Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.
  • As a direct download from us. Paddle, our merchant of record, would handle the purchase — your card and billing details, the receipt and tax — under Paddle's privacy policy. We never see card numbers.

Neither edition is on sale yet. Buying does not create an account with us.

This website

longroadsoftware.com is a static site served by GitHub Pages. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read, but it is off until you say yes: a notice at the bottom of the page asks until you answer, then not again, and nothing is loaded from Google and no cookie is set until you press Yes. No answer counts as no. Your answer is kept in your browser's local storage, not a cookie.

If you say yes, Google Analytics sends Google the page address, the site that linked you here, your browser and device type, and your approximate location, under Google's privacy policy. GitHub, as the host, keeps ordinary server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the request for the font files.
