An RV checklist app for iPhone and iPad.

Check & Roll walks you through setup, departure and maintenance in the right order, with no account and no Long Road Software server.

What it does

  • Built-in lists for travel trailers, fifth wheels, motorhomes, toads and tow vehicles.
  • Steps are presented in order: check or skip each one before moving on.
  • Critical steps, such as the black tank valve, ask you to confirm before you move on.
  • A hands-free walk-through reads each step aloud and listens for "done", so you can keep your hands on the job.
  • Works with Siri.
  • Works with no signal, anywhere you park.

Your information

  • No account.
  • No Long Road Software server. Nothing about your rig or your checklists is sent to us. Your checklists are kept on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.
