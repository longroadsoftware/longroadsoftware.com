In plain words: Check & Roll online manual with screenshots and step-by-step help.

Check & Roll is your RV checklist app. It helps you set up your daily inspection routine, walk through the process hands-free, and keep track of what matters. This manual shows you how to use every part of the app.

- [Run a Checklist](walkthrough.html) – Walk through your routine from start to finish  
- [Hands-Free Mode](siri.html) – Use Siri to trigger checklists  
- [iCloud Sync](icloud.html) – Keep your rigs synced across devices  
- [Share & Export](share-export.html) – Share lists with team members (coming soon)  
- [Apple Watch Companion](watch.html) – Get reminders on your watch
