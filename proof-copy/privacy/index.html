Proof Copy

Privacy

The short version: the Proof Copy app collects nothing. There is no account, and nothing is sent off your Mac. This page also says what this website and our email list keep.

The app

Proof Copy copies a card or a folder, reads the copy back, and checks that it matches the original, byte for byte. The report and the receipt it writes are saved in the copy, on your own drive. Your files, their names and what the report says are not sent anywhere. Checking a copy later reads the receipt on your Mac, and needs no account.

A licence key is checked on your Mac. It is not sent anywhere. Proof Copy is not on sale yet.

App Store edition: the app collects nothing. The purchase goes through Apple; we don't get your name or email from it.

The email list

If you leave your email on the Proof Copy page, the address goes to Kit, our email service, which keeps the list for us. Kit sends a message first to check the address is yours, and nothing is added until you click the link in it. We use the address only for Long Road Software news, and every email has an unsubscribe link. What Kit keeps, and how to be removed, is in the email list section of our main privacy page.

This website

longroadsoftware.com is a static site served by GitHub Pages. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read, but it is off until you say yes: a notice at the bottom of the page asks until you answer, then not again. Google Analytics is not loaded, and no analytics cookie is set, until you press Yes. No answer counts as no. Your answer is kept in your browser's local storage, not a cookie.

If you say yes, Google Analytics sends Google the page address, the site that linked you here, your browser and device type, and your approximate location, under Google's privacy policy.

Two other services see every visit, whatever you answer. GitHub, as the host, keeps ordinary server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. The page fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives the request for the font files.
